Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Short version: Loaf FM has no accounts and no ads. It uses Google Analytics to count visits. Your settings stay in your browser.

What's stored, and where

Your palette, sound, volume and brightness are saved in your browser's local storage on your device, so the board looks the same next time. We never receive them. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

If you install Loaf FM as an app, your browser also keeps a copy of the site's pages so it opens offline.

Your location

Loaf FM never asks for your location. The clock simply uses your device's own time and time zone.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they use. It sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to tell visits apart, except in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, where it runs without cookies. Google Analytics doesn't store IP addresses, advertising features are off, and we don't combine this with anything else. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Loaf FM doesn't load Google Analytics at all. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Services your browser talks to

Besides this website, only Google Analytics. The clock, the music and the sounds are all made on your device, and the fonts are served from loaffm.com.

Hosting

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and pages requested) for security and reliability. We don't use them to identify or track visitors.

Email

If you write to us, we'll use your email only to reply.

Children

Loaf FM doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes, for example if we add analytics, we'll update this page and the date above first.
