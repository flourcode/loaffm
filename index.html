Pet Cats and Chill

Loaf FM is a clock radio with a cat named Loafie living inside it.

Put on some music, rain, brown noise, ocean waves, a fireplace or just the purr. Leave it running while you work, study, read, meditate, browse Reddit or go down some random internet rabbit hole.

The music is made live in your browser. No playlists, no outside radio streams, no account.

Or make your own mixtape: pick a band, a mood and a groove, give it a name and send it to someone.

It's a tiny radio station you can mess with, and a cat hanging out on your desk.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. I like cats and lo-fi music, so I made this.

It's free. There are no ads and nothing to sign up for. I use Google Analytics to count visitors; the privacy page explains what gets collected.

If you like Loaf FM, send it to another cat person.

Make a mixtape

Hit the MIXTAPE button under the display, then pick:

Vibe
The band. Lo-fi, Bossa Nova, House, Ambient or 8-bit, each with its own instruments and rhythm.
Mood
Sunny, Moody or Late Night.
Groove
Lazy sits back, Pocket grooves, Move moves.
Sound
How it was recorded: Studio, Cassette, Vinyl, or from the Next Room.
Weather
Rain, ocean, a fire or a purr outside the window.

Loaf FM handles everything else.

Then give it a name. The name quietly writes the tune: every name has its own little hook that keeps coming back, so your tape sounds like itself every time, whichever band plays it.

Not sure where to start? Roll tape picks a random one. Keep rolling until something sounds right.

Share or Copy link puts the whole mixtape in a link you can send to someone. They'll hear the same tune and the same changes, played a little differently, because the band is playing it live. Download card gives you a picture of your tape to post with it. Mixtapes are kept in your browser, nowhere else, so the link is also your backup if you switch devices or clear your browser.

Made a good one? Send me the link at hello@loaffm.com. The best ones might end up playing on a Loaf FM stream.

Meet Loafie

Loafie wanders around, loafs, grooms herself, yawns, watches the weather and stares off into space.

Leave her alone for a while and she'll fall asleep. Come back and she'll eventually wake up and wander around again.

Pet her: hold on the cat and she'll close her eyes and purr. Keep going and she'll fall asleep.

Call her: tap the desk and you'll hear a quiet “pspsps.”

Feed her: tap the fish. She'll come over, eat it and purr.

She has a cardboard box too. Sometimes she gets in on her own. Tap the box to call her over. Tap it again while she's inside and she'll duck down.

Pick a station

Loaf FM has five, each on its own spot on the dial. The music is composed while you listen, so it changes as it plays.

Late Shift
Warm electric piano and a sparse beat. Good for working late.
Rainy Window
Soft keys, a little melody and rain. Good for studying, reading or getting something done.
CRT Dreams
Analog pads, a slow arpeggio and a few electronic blips. A little more retro and synthy.
Sunday Loaf
Jazz chords, brushes and a quiet purr underneath. The least urgent station.
Desert Night
Slow pads, distant bells and crickets. Good for reading, meditation or winding down.

Everything is composed and synthesised in your browser. There are no recordings, samples or third-party radio streams.

Or skip the music

There's also purr, rain, ocean waves, fireplace and brown noise.

Pick one, set the volume and leave it running.

Sleep timer

Like every clock radio, there's a SLEEP button. Tap it for 15, 30 or 60 minutes; when the time's up the music fades out. Tap again to change it or turn it off.

Twenty minutes

Hit the big Z for twenty quiet minutes.

Loafie curls up, the display dims and the dots along the bottom mark the time.

There's no guided meditation. Sit quietly, close your eyes, watch the cat or do whatever works for you.

A soft tone plays when the twenty minutes are up.

Loafie gets a fish.

Her sky

Stars come out at night and the moon shows its actual phase.

Winter gets snow. Spring gets petals. Summer nights get fireflies. Autumn gets falling leaves. Some days it rains.

Loaf FM doesn't need your location. It uses the date and time zone already on your device.

If you pick Rain or Rainy Window, it'll rain in her sky regardless.

Keep her in the corner

On a computer, hit the window button and Loaf FM floats over your other apps.

It's useful if you want the clock and cat sitting off to the side while you work, study or browse.

In Chrome and Edge you can still pet her, call her over, give her a fish or start a sit from the floating window.

Got an old tablet?

Loaf FM makes a pretty good desk clock.

Open it on the tablet, add it to the home screen, set the tablet to stay awake while charging and prop it up somewhere.

That's about it.

It works offline

You can install Loaf FM from your browser and run it like a small app without the rest of the website around it.

Once it's loaded, the cat, clock, stations and sounds work without an internet connection.

Nerd stuff

Loaf FM isn't playing a bunch of audio files.

The music is generated in real time with the Web Audio API. The synths, drums, bass, chord changes, melodies, rain, waves, fire and purr are made in your browser while you're listening.

There are no prerecorded songs, samples, Spotify embeds or internet radio streams.

It's procedural, not AI-generated.

Once the app is cached, most of it works offline. Your browser is basically the synthesizer.

A mixtape link is just a few choices and a name. Your browser plays the rest.

The whole thing is deliberately small, because a clock radio with a cat in it probably shouldn't need 40 MB of JavaScript.

There are no keyboard shortcuts, on purpose. You're probably typing in another window, and a stray key shouldn't stop the music.
