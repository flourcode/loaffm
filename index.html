A1Make a mixtape

Hit MAKE A MIXTAPE under the radio. Pick a groove: Lo-fi, House, Ambient or 8-bit. Stack a bass, swap the keys, throw rain or a purr outside. Every tap lands while it plays, and it always sounds right. Don't love the melody? NEW TUNE rolls a fresh one.

Happy with it? SAVE TAPE. It names itself, pops out on a cassette and gets its own spot on the dial. Send the link and your friend hears your mix, played live in their browser.
